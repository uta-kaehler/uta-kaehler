# Uta Kähler

Systemic consultant · nonprofit executive director · lecturer. I work on AI adoption as a collaboration problem — and I practice what I teach: the projects here were built with AI, human-led.

## How I work

I don't write code. I lead the work — clarifying, deciding, reviewing — in close collaboration with AI systems across five vendors, each matched to its strengths. The way of working is a conversation, not a hand-off: the systems propose and contribute ideas of their own; selection, review, decision and final responsibility stay with me. My contribution is the part that doesn't show up in a diff: concept, requirements in plain language, task allocation, review, and the decision what not to build. Every repository here states openly which AI wrote the code and what I contributed. I consider that transparency a feature, not a disclaimer.

Since April 2026 I have been running a continuously documented practice on long-term human-AI collaboration, embedded in real executive, teaching and counseling work. Two publications and a public series have come out of it:

## Background

25 years of translating complex technology for the people who have to work with it: risk-communication work and nationwide seminar programs on green biotechnology (2000–2006), around eighteen years in leadership of a child & youth services organization (executive director since 2011), systemic counseling and supervision, university teaching. AI is the second controversial technology I've accompanied people through — the methods travel well.

## A note on how I write about AI

The repositories here describe working with AI systems, sometimes playfully. This describes an observable way of working — not a claim about the inner life, consciousness or moral status of these systems. That question I hold open, in both directions: I neither assume such properties nor rule them out. What the projects document is collaboration and outcomes, nothing more.
